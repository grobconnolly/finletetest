What you're buying

A fixed share of one player's MLB paychecks

Not a loan. Not team equity. Not a collectible. A contract that pays you a percentage of a specific athlete's major-league earnings for the length of the payout term.

STEP 1

Pick a prospect

Every athlete on Finlete is in affiliated minor-league baseball and ranked among his organization's top prospects. You see his level, rank, stats and valuation before you buy.

STEP 2

Buy shares at his valuation

Your investment buys a proportional slice of his future MLB earnings. $300 minimum, zero fees at checkout, under three minutes, through DealMaker Securities, an SEC-registered broker-dealer.

STEP 3

Get paid when he does

Once he earns MLB income, dividends are paid twice a season, within 30 days after the All-Star Game and after the World Series, for a 25-year payout term.

How we pick and price

Fair means a solid big-league career returns your capital

Real prospects, not lottery tickets

Every athlete we sign is in affiliated minor-league baseball and ranked among his organization's top prospects. Most are already at Double-A or Triple-A, one or two steps from the majors.

Valuations set against real outcomes

We price each prospect so that a regular, multi-year MLB career returns investors' capital and a star career returns multiples. Zazueta at $6.8M is what that looks like: a top-30 prospect whose investors break even on a single arbitration-level season.

What can go wrong

Read this before you invest

We would rather you understand the downside here than discover it later. Only invest what you can afford to lose.

  • He may never reach the majors.Most minor leaguers don't. If he earns no MLB income, you receive nothing.
  • Injuries and release end careers early.A career that ends below the break-even number means you get back less than you invested.
  • Your shares are illiquid.There is a one-year lockup under Regulation CF and no public market. Plan to hold for the full payout term.
  • Dividends start only when MLB income starts.That can be years away, and the payout term runs 25 years.
  • Projections are not promises.Every multiple on this page is arithmetic on a valuation, not a forecast. Read the Form C and offering documents in full.
