Valuations

A valuation is a price. Here's what Zazueta's investors own.

Each offering sells a fixed share of one player's future MLB earnings at a stated valuation. Investors break even when his career earnings reach that number. Above it, every dollar is profit in proportion. If he never reaches MLB, the return is $0.

How we price. Every valuation is agreed with the athlete before his offering opens and shown on the offering page. The exact share of earnings sold and the price per share are in the Form C.

Zazueta signed in 2026 at $6.8M. He is now Baseball America's No. 24 prospect, was the Dodgers' 2025 Minor League Pitcher of the Year, and has been promoted to Double-A. His single offering is sold out; he remains one of six arms in Pitching Bundle 1.
