Fair valuations

We signed Christian Zazueta at a $6.8M valuation. Today he's a top-30 prospect in all of baseball.

A valuation is the price of 100% of a player's MLB earnings over the 25-year payout term. Your investment buys a proportional slice. Investors break even when his career earnings reach the valuation. Every dollar above it is profit, in proportion.

Multiples are what the valuation implies at each outcome, not results. Zazueta is in the minors and no dividends have been paid. If he never reaches MLB, the return is $0. His single offering is sold out; he remains one of six arms in Pitching Bundle 1.

How it works

Pick a prospect. Buy in at his valuation.

  1. 01

    Pick a prospect

    Finlete signs minor-league baseball prospects and prices each one at a valuation you can see before you buy. Team, level and prospect rank are shown on each athlete page.

  2. 02

    Buy in at his valuation

    From $300, with $0 checkout fees. Offerings are made under Regulation Crowdfunding and intermediated by DealMaker Securities, LLC, a FINRA/SIPC registered broker-dealer.

  3. 03

    Get paid if he reaches MLB

    Once he earns MLB income, dividends are paid twice a season, within 30 days after the All-Star Game and within 30 days after the World Series, for a 25-year term.
