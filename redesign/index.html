Questions before you invest

Short answers. Each offering’s Form C has the full terms.

What am I buying?

Shares issued by Finlete Funding, Inc. under Regulation Crowdfunding. Finlete holds a contract for a percentage of the athlete’s pre-tax, on-field MLB income and passes it on to shareholders. Each offering’s Form C describes the exact security.

What if the athlete never makes it?

You get nothing and lose your entire investment. The athlete owes nothing back. A short MLB career can also mean a loss.

Can I sell my shares?

Not easily. There is a one-year lockup, and no public market for the shares after it. Expect to hold for the full 25-year term.

Can I change my mind?

Yes. You can cancel for any reason until 48 hours before a closing, and your funds are refunded.
